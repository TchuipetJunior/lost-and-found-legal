# Privacy Policy — Lost & Found Cameroon (Development Version)

**Last updated:** May 25, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Lost & Found Cameroon WhatsApp service ("the Service", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information about its users.

This is a development-phase service operated by an independent developer based in Cameroon. The Service is being built and tested. Real users should expect occasional bugs and should not rely on the Service for critical needs at this stage.

## 1. Information we collect

When you message our WhatsApp number, we automatically receive:
- Your WhatsApp phone number
- Your WhatsApp display name (as set by you on WhatsApp)
- The content of the messages you send to us (text, images, locations)
- Metadata such as message timestamps

When you use the Service to declare a found object or search for a lost object, we additionally collect:
- The information you voluntarily provide about the object (names, dates of birth, photos of identification documents, location of public deposit points)
- Mobile money payment information you provide during payment or withdrawal

## 2. How we use this information

We use the information for the sole purpose of operating the Lost & Found Service, specifically:
- Matching found objects with their owners
- Notifying users of relevant matches
- Processing payments and finder rewards
- Maintaining a record of transactions for accounting and audit purposes
- Resolving disputes between users or with our administrators

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 3. Service providers

We rely on third-party providers to operate the Service. These providers process data on our behalf:
- **Meta Platforms (WhatsApp)** for message delivery
- **Supabase** for database and file storage
- **MyCoolPay** for payment processing
- **OpenAI / Anthropic** for AI-assisted document understanding (vision OCR and intent classification)

Each of these providers operates under its own privacy policy. We minimize the data shared with each.

## 4. Photos of identity documents

We treat photos of identity documents (national identity cards, driver's licenses, passports, student cards) with particular care:
- Photos are stored in a private storage bucket; URLs are time-limited and never public
- Sensitive numbers (ID numbers) are blurred in any image shared with a third party who is not the document owner
- Photos are automatically deleted from our storage 30 days after the object is collected by its owner

## 5. Data retention

User accounts and transaction records are retained for the duration of the user's relationship with the Service, plus an additional period required for accounting and dispute resolution (typically up to 5 years).

You may request deletion of your account at any time by messaging the agent with the request, or by contacting us at the address below.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to:
- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your information (subject to our retention obligations)
- Withdraw consent to the processing of your information

To exercise these rights, send a request to the contact address below.

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard technical measures to protect your information, including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), private storage buckets, signed and time-limited URLs for media access, and access controls on administrative operations.

No system is perfectly secure. Should a data breach occur, we will notify affected users as soon as reasonably possible.

## 8. Children

The Service is not intended for use by children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy as the Service evolves toward public launch. Material changes will be communicated to users through the WhatsApp channel.
